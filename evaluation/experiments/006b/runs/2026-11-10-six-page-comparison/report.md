**Recommendation, not implementation:** Docling's observed chart/table recognition, lower false-heading count and better region/order coverage justify its dependencies **as an optional layout candidate for further controlled hybrid investigation**. Its 103 packages, ~1.319 GB installed allocation, model storage and higher memory demand are substantial, and its text/region granularity and grounding remain unsuitable as unquestioned authority. Retain Structure 0.2 and the original character layer now. A future hybrid could treat Docling boxes/roles/order as fallible hypotheses while preserving deterministic IDs, Unicode and ambiguity. Independent human review and a separately approved validation design should precede adoption. No hybrid, additional benchmark, annotation correction or production change is implemented. **Stop after Stage D.**
